KoreaLink (the “Service”, operated by REPLACE_OPERATOR_NAME, “we”) is a community app for visitors to Korea. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you sign up with email, Google or Kakao, why we use it, how long we keep it, and how it is destroyed.

2. Purposes of use

  • Creating and identifying your account, signing you in (email, Google, Kakao).
  • Providing the Service: posts, comments, chat, meetups and companions, profiles shown to other travelers.
  • Keeping the community safe: handling reports, blocking abuse, preventing spam (rate limits).
  • Responding to your requests, such as account deletion or questions.

We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising or marketing.

3. What other users can see

Your username, display name, nationality (flag), country of residence, gender, bio and profile photo are always shown on your public profile. Your birth year (shown as age) is shown only if you turn it on. Your email address is never shown to other users. Posts, comments, public chat messages and meetups you create are visible to others; meetup group chats are visible only to confirmed participants and the host.

4. Retention and destruction

  • We keep your information while your account exists.
  • When you withdraw (delete your account) in the app, your account, profile, social-login link, posts, comments, likes, chat messages, meetups you hosted, join records, uploaded photos, and reports about your content are deleted immediately from our database and storage. Electronic files are deleted so that they cannot be restored.
  • Backup copies, if any, are removed automatically when the infrastructure provider’s backup cycle expires. Server access logs kept by our providers are retained only for a short period under their own policies.
  • If a law requires us to keep certain information, we keep only that information, separately, for the period required by that law, then destroy it.

6. Your rights

  • View and correct your information: Me → Edit profile in the app.
  • Delete your account and data: Me → Delete account in the app (see how to delete your account, which also works without logging in).
  • You can also remove the app’s access in your Google or Kakao account settings.
  • Other requests (access, suspension of processing): email us at the address below. We respond without delay and within 10 days.

7. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 14 has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed; connections use HTTPS; database access is restricted by row-level security so users can only change their own content; secret keys are never placed in the app. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong password and meet other travelers in public places.
